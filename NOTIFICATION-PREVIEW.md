# Kartik Tracker — Reminder Notification Preview

**Journey dates:** 26 October–24 November 2026 (Asia/Kolkata).

Notifications are sent only to a devotee who has enabled reminders and has a valid subscribed device. At the selected local reminder time, the scheduler sends a reminder only if at least one active vow is still incomplete. The title uses the day number, and the body shows how many vows remain plus up to the first three incomplete vow names. Tapping the notification opens the Kartik tracker.

## Example: daily reminder (English)

**Title**

> 🪔 Hare Krishna — Day 1 vows pending

**Body**

> 3 of 12 vows are still incomplete today: Offer a ghee lamp to Lord Damodara, Recite Damodarastakam, Offer water to Tulasi Maharani…. Please complete them before you rest.

## Example: दैनिक रिमाइंडर (हिन्दी)

**शीर्षक**

> 🪔 हरे कृष्ण प्रभु जी — दिन 1 के व्रत शेष हैं

**संदेश**

> आज 12 में से 3 व्रत अभी पूरे नहीं हुए: भगवान दामोदर को घी का दीप अर्पित करें, दामोदराष्टकम का पाठ करें, तुलसी महारानी को जल अर्पित करें…। विश्राम से पहले इन्हें पूरा करें।

*Note:* The exact greeting (Hare Krishna / Prabhuji / Mataji), day number, total vows, pending count, and vow names depend on the devotee's profile and tracker. Only the first three pending vows are listed; an ellipsis indicates more. If all active vows are complete, no daily reminder is sent.

## Example: test notification after enabling reminders

**English**

> 🔔 Hare Krishna — reminders are ON
>
> Test successful. If any vow is incomplete, you will get a reminder at 6:00 PM.

**हिन्दी**

> 🔔 हरे कृष्ण प्रभु जी — रिमाइंडर चालू हो गया
>
> परीक्षण सफल रहा। यदि आज कोई व्रत अधूरा होगा, तो 6:00 PM पर रिमाइंडर मिलेगा।

The test message's displayed time changes to match the time selected in the app (for example, selecting 10:46 AM displays 10:46 AM, and selecting 3:15 PM displays 3:15 PM). The times above are examples only. The scheduled reminder itself is sent only during the Kartik date window and only when at least one active vow is still incomplete.

## Example: individual vow reminder (1.2.0)

**Title:** 🪔 Hare Krishna — Day 3 vow reminder

**Body:** Time for: Recite Damodarastakam. It isn’t marked complete yet.

Vows due in the same minute are grouped ("2 vows are due now: …"). Tapping opens the Daily tab.
